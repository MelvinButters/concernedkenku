Magic Item Cards

Make cards for your magic items that fit a standard sleeve (63.5 × 88.9 mm). Nine print per A4 sheet, in 3 columns and 3 rows. Click a card to flip between its black back and the item face.

Click a card's art box to upload a photo, or click a card then paste (Ctrl/Cmd+V) an image anywhere on the page. It fills whichever card you last clicked.

Export to PDF lays every card out 3×3 (9 per A4 page) at exact sleeve size and downloads a print-ready file, so there are no browser print settings to fuss with.
